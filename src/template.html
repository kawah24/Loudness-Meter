Only the container's metadata is rewritten — the audio payload is copied byte for byte, so a WAV stays sample-identical and an MP3/FLAC keeps its original frames. “Strip all” removes BWF/bext, iXML, XMP, ID3, APE and cover art.

How this meter works

M — Momentary400 ms window, 100 ms hop. What you feel moment to moment.
S — Short-term3 s window. Loudness of a phrase or a scene.
I — IntegratedGated average over everything measured: −70 LUFS absolute gate, then −10 LU relative gate (BS.1770-4).
LRALoudness range, EBU Tech 3342: P95 − P10 of short-term loudness after a −20 LU gate.
True peak4× oversampled inter-sample peak in dBTP. Ceiling is usually −1.0 dBTP.
PLR / PSRPeak-to-loudness and peak-to-short-term ratio: how much crest factor the mix keeps.
Space play / pause
